RetiLink

A retinal photo in primary care should end in a specialist's signed answer.

Most portable eye screens stop at a result nobody acts on. RetiLink turns the photo into an accountable handoff between clinicians, and tracks it until the loop is closed.

  1. {{ icon('eye') }}
    Screen
    Quality gate and referable-DR model on each eye, with attention maps.
  2. {{ icon('shield') }}
    Review and sign
    The referring clinician accepts or overrides the model and signs their own interpretation.
  3. {{ icon('send') }}
    Consult
    A traced package with guideline evidence goes to a specialist, who must acknowledge it.
  4. {{ icon('calendar') }}
    Close the loop
    The coordinator schedules and removes barriers; the referrer closes on a signed response.

Research prototype. Synthetic patients, simulated workflow history. Models trained on mBRSET (portable camera, people with diabetes).
