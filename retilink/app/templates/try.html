What you get

  • Retina: photo quality, referable diabetic retinopathy, grade probabilities, macular edema, and an attention map per photo.
  • Whole body: all nine systemic conditions scored, each labelled research, exploratory or near chance, with the evidence behind it.
  • Why: for each condition, how much the retinal images and each patient detail pushed the score up or down.

The models were trained on mBRSET: a handheld smartphone camera, dilated pupils, adults with diabetes in Brazil. Other cameras are a domain shift, so treat scores on them as exploratory. Sample photos with labels are in test_data/ on the research machine.
