What to expect

The models were trained on mBRSET: a handheld smartphone fundus camera, dilated pupils, adults with diabetes in Brazil. Other cameras and populations are a domain shift, so treat scores on them as exploratory.

  • Colour fundus photographs work best: macula- or disc-centred, the whole round field of view visible.
  • Public datasets for testing include APTOS 2019, Messidor-2, IDRiD and EyePACS; check each licence first.
  • Phone photos of a face or eye are rejected by the suitability check, by design.
