Where the field is going

Published research, not Insight Rx results. It is why Insight Rx is built to add organ systems as each one passes its own validation gate.

  • Poplin et al., Nature Biomedical Engineering 2018Deep learning estimated cardiovascular risk factors such as age, blood pressure and smoking status from retinal photographs.

  • Zhou et al., Nature 2023 (RETFound)A retinal foundation model adapted to ocular and systemic prediction tasks; Insight Rx uses it for systemic association features.
