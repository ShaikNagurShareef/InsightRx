Where the data comes from

  • Structures: AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (DeepMind and EMBL-EBI, CC-BY 4.0) and experimental drug complexes from the RCSB Protein Data Bank.
  • Drugs on each target: ChEMBL mechanism records (EMBL-EBI), with approval phase.
  • Therapy options and interactions: curated from ADA, AAO, KDIGO guidelines and FDA labels, with every source cited on the card.
  • Trials and physicians: ClinicalTrials.gov and the CMS NPPES NPI Registry, queried live with an offline snapshot as fallback.
