Insight Rx

Oculomics to therapy, at the point of care

From one eye photo to the right therapy and the right physician.

A retinal photo takes seconds and needs no needle. Insight Rx screens it for diabetic eye disease, reads what it says about the heart, kidneys and nerves, then connects that signal to guideline therapy, the molecular targets behind it, recruiting trials, and the specialist or manufacturer medical team who can answer.
