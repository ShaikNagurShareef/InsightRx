Why I built this

I research oculomics. The retina is the one place where we can look straight at blood vessels and nerves, with no needle and no scan.

In most clinics that photo ends as a line in a chart. Insight Rx makes it start a treatment plan.

Key differentiators · what is new

Screening tools stop at "refer".
Insight Rx goes to the molecule, for each patient.

1 Eye to protein targetA retinal phenotype ranked into protein targets per patient, with every rank explained.
2 Site-level structure confidenceAlphaFold pLDDT measured at the exact drug-contact residues from real PDB complexes.
3 Eye-aware drug safetyDrug-disease alerts tied to what the retina shows: semaglutide with DR, pioglitazone with edema.
4 Signal-triggered engagementConsults, NPI referral and manufacturer med-info start from a clinical finding, behind a firewall.
5 Honest by designQuality-gated, calibrated models with evidence tiers; research signals are never sold as diagnoses.
6 Real-world data, free to runPortable-camera patients from Brazil; runs on free infrastructure with a live GPU worker.
